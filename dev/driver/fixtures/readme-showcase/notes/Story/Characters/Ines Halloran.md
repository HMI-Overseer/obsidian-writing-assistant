# Ines Halloran

Hauler pilot, Ceres to Saturn and back, eleven runs so far. Bored by the belt, frightened of the rings, and has never told anyone the second part.

- Counts down burns aloud. A habit from her instructor, Reyes.
- Keeps a chip of ring ice in a vial on the console. It is the only thing she has ever stolen.
