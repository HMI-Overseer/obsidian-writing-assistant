# Premise

Ice harvesters on Daphnis Station, inside the Keeler Gap at the outer edge of Saturn's A ring. The station shepherds a strip of ring the way the moon does, and sells water to everything from Ceres outward.

Ines Halloran flies the supply run from Ceres. Chapter one is her last outbound trip before the station's contract lapses and the strip goes back to the ring.

Working title: *Shepherd*.
